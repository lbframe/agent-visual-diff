# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple standard: be respectful, technically constructive, and focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, or deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate this standard and may restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and productive.

When disagreeing, critique the implementation, evidence, or trade-off rather than the person.
